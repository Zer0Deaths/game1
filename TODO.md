Claude is building the game; these steps need your Roblox account, Studio, or money.
Tick them off as you go. Nothing here is urgent unless marked.

## Studio settings
- [ ] **Max players per server: 16.** Game Settings > Places > (your place) > Server Size... or Home > Game Settings > Places, set *Max Players* to 16. (Scripts can't change this.)

## Play-test checklist (Studio: Play for solo, or Test > Clients and Servers with 2-4)
- [ ] Safehouse: press Ready at the descent lift. "GOING DOWN 5" on the HUD, then you land on sublevel 1 (Output: `[Level] crew 1, sublevel 1 ...`).
- [ ] HUD shows your crew, sublevel and `$banked / $quota`. Deposit at the lift until the quota is met: the lift turns green and the HUD says TO THE LIFT.
- [ ] Stand in the lift: the panel offers Go deeper (E) and Return to safehouse (R). Deeper builds sublevel 2 (Lampjaw shows up from here); Return takes you to the safehouse with your Cash.
- [ ] Let the timer run out (or set `SUBLEVEL_SECONDS` to 60 in Config to test): the crew is wiped, back in the safehouse, shop kit gone.
- [ ] Two players in different crews: each crew gets its own level and never sees the other's.
- [ ] The Tenant shows up after the first Shift (tick `ForceShift`), follows your noise, and the lift is safe.
- [ ] Map (M) fills in and fades after a Shift.
- [ ] **Blender models:** Output says `[Meshes] 65 models loaded` a few seconds after Play (once the PC has uploaded the props batch; fewer before that). Check that loot shows its model (and the white outline when you look at it), the tube fittings switch off in a Shift blackout, the lockers have crates on top, and desks, chairs, pillars, door frames, vents and pipes look right. Anything floating, sunk or turned the wrong way: tell Claude which piece. If a line says `came in turned`, mention that too.
- [ ] **Everything modelled (the props batch, after the PC uploads it):** the walls are panels with a moulded skirting, a post wherever walls turn or end, and the doorways cut through; wallpaper peels off here and there on The Carpet. The floor is carpet tiles with seams (a few lifting or missing), the ceiling is tiles in their grid with the odd tile hanging down out of a black hole. Every prop is a model now: cabinets, racks with archive boxes and cartons, coolers, bins, binders, monitors and keyboards (Carpet); trolleys, drips, wheelchairs, bay curtains, medicine cabinets, handrails (Ward); drums, boilers, pumps, pipe drops with valves, junction boxes, cable trays (Plant); socket plates, notice boards, the exit lift's cage. In the safehouse: carpet tiles, the counter, wardrobe, supply locker, filing cabinet and the descent lift (same places as before). Check: nothing floats, sinks or faces the wrong way, you can't see through the floor or the ceiling anywhere, you still bump into what you see, and lights and Shifts still work. Two numbers to tell Claude: the Shift line in Output (`[Shift] ... rebuilt in N ms`) and your frame rate standing in a big room. If either is bad, Claude thins the posts and panels out.
- [ ] **The Tenant, retuned (27 Nov, after the PC uploads `tenant` again):** it has a bone-white face now (two uneven sockets, a small open mouth) that catches a faint light, so you can make it out in the dark. It only sees what's in front of it. When it spots you it stops, lifts that face at you and screeches for 2.5 seconds before it moves: that's your cue to run. Duck out of sight in that time and it doesn't chase; it walks to where it saw you, stands and listens. When it does chase, it builds up speed and on sublevel 1 runs about as fast as you walk, so a sprint always gets away. Lose it round a corner and it slows to a walk, goes to where it last saw you and listens before following sounds, a step behind. Tell Claude if it's now too easy or still too hard; every number is in `Config.TENANT`. Until the upload, the old body has no face: Output says `[Meshes] tenant: only 15 of 17 meshes found`, which is expected.
- [ ] **The Quiet** (sublevel 3 and down, or anywhere with the `ForceQuiet` Workspace attribute: tick it while you're in a level). Output: `[Quiet] crew 1: the hum stops around <you>`. The hum sags and drains away over 2 seconds, the tubes around you dim, then dead silence and one slow breath just behind you. Stand still (looking around is fine) and after 4-6 seconds the hum comes back and the tubes stutter up. Walk, jump or drop loot in the silence and you're taken. The first time it also says "The hum has stopped. Don't move." Tell Claude if the warning feels too short or the breath sounds wrong.
- [ ] **The Foreman** (sublevel 4 and down, or anywhere with the `ForceForeman` Workspace attribute: tick it while you're in a level; it jumps the timer to 90 s). The lift dings, the banner says it's switching off the level, and after 5 seconds the far end starts going dark, breaker by breaker, closing in on the lift. Open the map (M): the dark cells are blacked out. Walk back and it never reaches you; stand still out in the level and when your cell goes dark you're taken. Output: `[Foreman] crew 1: switching off from N cells out`. Until the PC uploads `foreman` its body is built from blocks (hat over a black hole, keys on the belt); after the upload, Output says `[EntityRig] Foreman: the Blender model`.
- [ ] **The bigger lift and its armoury.** The exit lift is now 16 x 16 studs with an ARMOURY cabinet and pegboard on its west side. Walk up to it and press E: the LIFT ARMOURY screen sells four melee weapons for your Cash (lead pipe $90, crowbar $180, fire axe $320, sledgehammer $450). You hold one at a time; it shows bottom right with its hits left. Click (R2 on a pad) to swing: only the weapon moves, never your view. Hit the Tenant and it freezes for 2.5-6.5 s (Output: `[Melee] <you> stunned the Tenant`), then wanders off dazed. Hit Lampjaw's red light and it smashes (`[Lampjaw] ... smashed`). Every swing is loud: the Tenant hears it, and in the Quiet's silence it kills you. Die and you lose the weapon. Until the PC uploads the new models the weapons and cabinet are built from blocks, and the lift cage is plain posts (the old 10 x 10 cage mesh isn't used on the bigger lift). Tell Claude if the swing feels slow or the weapon sits wrong in view.
- [ ] **Finding the lift on sublevels 1-3.** The lift starts much closer to where you land (about a fifth of the way across on sublevel 1, a third by sublevel 3), a Shift only moves it a short walk away, and the compass needle holds steady. On sublevels 1 and 2 a faint `[ LIFT ]` marker shows through the walls. From sublevel 4 it's back to the old trek.
- [ ] **A new place every sublevel at the start.** Sublevel 1 is The Carpet, then 2 and 3 are The Ward and The Plant Room in either order, so the first three are always three different places. After that it never repeats the sublevel before. The banner names each one.
- [ ] **Audio, when you're ready to un-pause it:** three new sounds, all our own synthesis: the Quiet's breath (`QUIET_INHALE`), and the Foreman's keys (`FOREMAN_KEYS`) and whistle (`FOREMAN_WHISTLE`) in `assets/sounds/`. Until they're uploaded, slowed whispers stand in for the breath, and the Foreman is heard by its boots and the breakers only. They go up with `python tools/upload_assets.py --audio`, so only when you say audio is OK again.

## Uploads (done 27 Nov: 99 assets uploaded, IDs in `src/shared/AssetIds.luau`)
- [ ] **Props batch (the PC session does this):** `python tools/upload_assets.py` without `--audio` uploads the 31 new models and the two that changed (`floor_panel`, `ceiling_tile`), then commits `src/shared/AssetIds.luau` and `assets/uploaded.json`. Until then those props stay as blocks; the old floor and ceiling meshes stand in.
- [ ] **After the realism pass:** the PC session runs `python tools/upload_assets.py` once more (it only uploads the 10 new grime decals in `assets/decals`) and commits `src/shared/AssetIds.luau` and `assets/uploaded.json`. Until then the stains use the old flat patches.
- [ ] **Install the textures once (about 1 minute).** This same line also turns on the best lighting your Studio allows (Future, or Realistic + Prioritize Lighting Quality in newer Studios), which a running game can't do. A running game isn't allowed to create textured materials, so this one step happens in Studio itself:
  1. Make sure the game is **stopped** (not playing).
  2. Open **View > Command Bar**, paste this and press Enter:
     `require(game.ReplicatedStorage.Shared.Surfaces).install()`
  3. **File > Save** (so the materials are kept in the place).
  4. Play. Output should say `[Surfaces] 19 of 19 PBR sets active`. The command bar also prints `[Surfaces] lighting: ...` with what it could set.
  Do it again only after new textures are uploaded.
- To re-upload after changing files: `python tools/upload_assets.py` (skips anything unchanged), then commit `src/shared/AssetIds.luau` and `assets/uploaded.json`.
- Audio is moderated: a new sound can show "not approved" for a while after upload. It starts working by itself once Roblox approves it.
